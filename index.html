Roomwise Privacy Policy

Effective date: 13 November 2026

Who provides Roomwise

Roomwise: Room Planner (com.edinramovic.roomwise) is provided by Edin Ramovic, shown on Google Play under the developer name Ramovic Digital. For privacy questions or deletion requests, contact roomwise.app@gmail.com.

Your account and saved rooms

Firebase Authentication processes your name, email, password and account identifier to create and secure your account. Roomwise saves room plans, analyses and app-owned photo copies on your device, separated by account. This version does not provide cloud photo storage or cross-device room syncing. Signing out keeps that account’s local rooms for its next sign-in. Uninstalling the app or clearing its storage can remove local-only work.

Device backups and transfers

Depending on your Android and device settings, the operating system may back up or transfer app data, including local records and photo copies. Manage these backups through your device or backup-provider settings. Deletion in Roomwise clears the active data described below; Roomwise does not control copies retained by a device backup.

Optional AI features

When you choose photo analysis (“Get photo ideas”), the selected photos and your design goal are sent over encrypted connections through our Google Firebase Cloud Function to OpenAI. When you choose Optimize (“Suggest a layout”), room dimensions, furniture, modeled openings and the design instruction are sent through the same service. These providers process the request to return suggestions. Manual planning and fit checks do not send your room layout to OpenAI. Avoid submitting people, identifying documents or other information you do not want processed.

Provider processing and security

We use Google Firebase and OpenAI as service providers. We do not sell personal information or use it for advertising. OpenAI states that API content is not used for model training by default; its ordinary abuse-monitoring logs may retain content for up to 30 days, with policy and legal exceptions. Roomwise does not claim Zero Data Retention. Providers also process technical and security information under their service terms. We do not intentionally write your photos or full AI responses to our application logs.

Usage limits and reports

We store your account identifier, the latest usage date and counts to enforce the shared daily AI allowance and prevent abuse. These counters are removed with account deletion or by the daily cleanup once their latest date is more than 30 days old. If you submit an AI-output report, we receive the displayed AI text, your chosen reason, optional comment, account identifier and submission time. No photos are automatically attached. Reports help us review unsafe or inaccurate output and improve safeguards. Reports are eligible for deletion after 30 days and removed by the next daily cleanup, or with account deletion, subject to any retention required by law.

Delete your account and data

Use Profile → Delete account and confirm your password. The deletion process removes your Firebase account, Roomwise usage/report records and this account’s local room records and app-owned photo copies on this installation. Originals in your photo library are not deleted. Other offline installations may still hold local copies; clear Roomwise storage or uninstall it there. We retain a minimal account-deletion record to reject old access tokens and support cleanup retries. It expires after 30 days and is removed by the next daily cleanup. Provider security or legal records follow the retention described above. We do not promise deletion of backups on a schedule that has not been verified.

Request deletion without the app

Email roomwise.app@gmail.com from your account email with the subject “Roomwise account deletion”. We will verify control of the account and process the request. Never send your password or verification codes. For records stored only on your device, clear the app’s storage or uninstall it. Contact the same address if a deletion attempt fails or you need help with retained data.

Earlier beta data

Earlier beta versions saved local room records without a verified account owner. Those records are kept separate and are not shown to any signed-in account. You can discard them explicitly from Profile, or remove them by clearing app storage or uninstalling. Roomwise does not silently assign them to a new account.

Children and policy updates

Roomwise is not directed to children under 13. If you believe a child has provided personal information, contact us. We will update this policy and its date when our practices change. The policy link and support address are available below.
